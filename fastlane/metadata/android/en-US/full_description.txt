Loopky is a free, open source flashcard app with spaced repetition. Import your Anki decks, make your own, or study decks other people have shared.

<b>Import from Anki</b>
Open .apkg files, images included, and start studying. .txt and .csv files work too, or paste a word list and each line becomes a card.

<b>Spaced repetition you control</b>
Hard, Good and Easy bring a card back in 1, 3 and 7 days. Change those numbers in settings and every review uses them. New cards have a daily goal of 20, and reviews are never capped.

<b>Ways to study</b>
• Flip: tap to see the answer
• Type the answer, accents included
• Listen: hear the card read aloud
• Speak: say the answer and have it checked
• Reverse: get asked the other way round
Listen and Speak work in 35 languages.

<b>Shared decks</b>
Find decks by tag, follow the ones you like, or copy one and make it yours. Share your own deck with a link, handy for a class or a study group.

<b>No email, no password</b>
You sign in with a Pubky key. Your decks and review history live on a homeserver you control, so they follow you to a new phone or tablet. Published decks are public. No ads, no analytics.

Good for vocabulary, language learning, medical school and exam prep. Works on phones and tablets.
